# Brag Plan: SplitBiller

## What is this app?
A no-login bill splitter that turns a pile of group expenses into "who owes whom", then lets you settle through UPI in rupees.

## The angle
Everyone has been the person asking "wait, who owes whom?" in a group chat. SplitBiller answers it in seconds, with no signup and no app download. The video is the working flow sped up: people in, expenses in, two payments out. The product's own copy does the talking.

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
Giant serif "Who owes whom?" on the paper background, with a purple underline drawing in beneath "whom".

## Key moments (the middle)
- Four members (Rohan, Priya, Aisha, Dev) pop in one by one: "Add people involved in the expense".
- Expenses land row by row while the Goa trip total counts up to ₹38,460 over 11 expenses: "Enter each expense and who paid".
- The settle-up card from the landing page: Rohan → Priya ₹6,215, Aisha → Priya ₹2,940. A cursor presses "Pay ₹6,215 with UPI". Badge: "2 payments settle it".

## Outro / punchline
"Split bills in ₹, settle with UPI" with the three chips from the hero (Free forever · No sign-up · Takes ~30 seconds), then the SplitBiller logo.

## User flow worth showing
1. Add people → 2. Enter each expense and who paid → 3. See who owes whom and pay via UPI. This is the site's own 4-step "how it works" list, collapsed to three beats.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: quiet, confident product film with a small wink at the group-chat question
- Interpretation: smooth slides, clean cards, generous holds on every line, a bright upbeat bed. No chaos.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 22s

- Accent: #53307b (brand), soft #ece3f5

- Body font: Outfit
- Strongest visual element: the settle-up card with the purple "Pay ₹6,215 with UPI" button

## Share copy (draft)
Who owes whom? SplitBiller answers it in seconds. Split bills in ₹, settle with UPI. No sign-up, no app download.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse, motion-matched accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`, upbeat, 120 BPM
- Music treatment: fade in over the first second, sit at a low bed level, fade out through the final 2s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read. Beat grid every ~0.5s from 3.02s. Strong cues used: 16.02s (settle badge), 18.02s (outro title). Text rows snap to every other beat (1s apart) so they stay readable.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; music bass makes the background brand glow breathe and the settle card gain slight presence. No waveform or equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched: soft clicks for chips, card slide for expense rows, a click for the UPI button, a bell on the settle badge and the logo
- Audio-coupled moments: chips arriving one by one, counter ticking up, cursor press, final logo
- Restraint rule: nothing loud; SFX stay under the music bed

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 3.5s
"Who owes whom?" in Fraunces, centered. Underline sweeps under "whom". Small label above: "SplitBiller".
Sequential/interaction: none. Hold the headline fully settled for 2s+.
Audio intent: music fades in, one soft impact as the line lands.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Add people — 5s (3.5–8.5)
Step label "1 · Add people involved in the expense". Four member chips (Rohan, Priya, Aisha, Dev) pop in one by one on beats 4.02, 4.52, 5.02, 5.52. All four hold until the scene ends.
Sequential/interaction: yes — chips arrive one by one, click SFX each (names are single words, fine on every beat).
Transition mood: slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Enter expenses — 5s (8.5–13.5)
Step label "2 · Enter each expense and who paid". Group header "Goa trip · 4 members · 11 expenses". Three expense rows slide in at 9.02, 10.02, 11.02 (every other beat): Hotel ₹18,000 paid by Priya; Scooters ₹4,200 paid by Rohan; Dinner ₹3,600 paid by Aisha. Total counts up to ₹38,460.
Sequential/interaction: yes — rows one by one with card-slide SFX; counter ticks.
Transition mood: slide → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Settle up — 4.5s (13.5–18)
Step label "3 · Instantly see who owes whom and how much". The settle-up card from the landing page (Rohan → Priya ₹6,215, Aisha → Priya ₹2,940). "2 payments settle it" badge locks to the 16.02s strong cue. A cursor moves to "Pay ₹6,215 with UPI" and presses at 17.02s.
Sequential/interaction: yes — rows then badge; simulated click.
Transition mood: wipe → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 4s (18–22)
"Split bills in ₹, settle with UPI" lands on 18.02s strong cue. Three chips: Free forever · No sign-up · Takes ~30 seconds. Logo + "SplitBiller" and "A Splitwise alternative without the login." Hold to the end.
Audio intent: bell on the title, music fades out.

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat, warm
**Audio summary:** a light fade-in, tick-tick-tick as people join, a card slide per expense, a click on pay, a bell on the payoff, a gentle fade-out.

Names and amounts (Rohan, Priya, Aisha, Goa trip, ₹38,460, ₹6,215, ₹2,940) come from the landing page's own settle-up preview. Dev and the three expense rows are invented stand-ins; the rows are illustrative and do not claim to reproduce the app's math. No user data appears.
